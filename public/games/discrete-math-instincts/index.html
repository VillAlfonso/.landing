Discrete Math Instincts

Use the values at the top to evaluate the blue expression closest to the bottom. Select True (A) or False (D) before it falls past the answer line. A wrong or missed answer costs a heart.

Binary expressions earn 2 points; negation earns 1. Seven correct answers in a row restore one heart, or earn a bonus point with full health. Extra lanes unlock at 8 and 20 points. In Advanced mode, the truth values change every 10 points.

-p means NOT p. Operators:

pqp v qp ^ qp → qp ↔ q
TTTTTT
TFTFFF
FTTFTF
FFFFTT

The game pauses while these rules are open.
