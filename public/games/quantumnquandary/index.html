Browser recreation of the original C# console game, using its ASCII artwork. Original team credits are preserved in the menu. Touch controls appear below the terminal; player choices stay hidden until both lock in.

Charge. Defend. Counter.

Both players start with 2 charges. Choose one action each round. Unaffordable actions are disabled. Matching actions and successful blocks continue the battle.

  • Charge: gain 1 charge.
  • Surrender: block a Gun without spending charge.
  • Mirror: spend 3 to reflect Gun or Kamehameha.
  • Gun: spend 2 to defeat Charge.
  • Kamehameha: spend 5 to defeat Charge, Surrender, or Gun.
  • Nuclear Bomb: spend 8 to defeat any other action.

Player 1: Q / W / E / A / S / D.
Player 2: I / O / P / J / K / L.
